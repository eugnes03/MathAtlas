Algebraic Topology / Definition
Fundamental Group
Let f be a loop and [f] its homotopy equivalence class. We define π₁(X,x) to be the set of equivalence classes of loops based at x — this is the fundamental group of X.
Margin note: this is the starting point for covering space theory.
Builds on Loops and Homotopy, by way of Homotopy Equivalence Class.
Leads to Dependence on the Base Point and the Fundamental Group Axiom Verification.
